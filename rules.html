Rules

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The short version

List something real, honest and safe. Visitors click your link trusting that it is what you say it is. We remove listings that break that trust.

1. What you may list

  • A working website, app or page that you own or have the right to promote.
  • A name and one-line description that accurately describe what visitors will find there.
  • A link that goes straight to the thing you describe.

2. What you may not list

The following are not allowed, whether in the link, the name, or the description:

  • Illegal content or activity, including the sale of illegal goods or services, and anything that breaks the laws of India or the place where the content is hosted.
  • Scams and fraud, such as fake giveaways, pyramid or get-rich-quick schemes, fake investment or “guaranteed return” offers, and impersonation of a person, brand or authority.
  • Malware and phishing, including sites that try to install software without clear consent, steal passwords, card details or personal information, or trick visitors into handing them over.
  • Sexually explicit or adult content, and anything that sexualises or exploits minors. The latter will also be reported to the authorities.
  • Hateful, abusive or violent content, including content that attacks people because of who they are, threatens harm, or promotes terrorism or self-harm.
  • Content that infringes others’ rights, such as pirated material, counterfeit goods, or the use of someone’s trademark to mislead.
  • Sites that expose private information about people without their consent.
  • Unlicensed or unlawful gambling, and prohibited financial products or services.

3. Honest names and descriptions

  • Do not use misleading claims, false statistics, fake testimonials, or promises you cannot keep.
  • Do not use someone else’s name or brand to suggest a connection that does not exist.
  • Keep descriptions plain: no excessive capital letters, repeated symbols, or spam-style text.
  • Do not put contact details, phone numbers or other links in the name or description fields.

4. Your link must stay honest

  • Do not redirect visitors to a different site from the one you described, or change what the link leads to after you are listed.
  • Do not use pop-ups, forced downloads, or tricks that make it hard for visitors to leave.
  • A link that stops working for a long time may be removed.

5. Fair play

  • Do not try to break, overload or exploit the Site, or to change your rank by any method other than a genuine payment.
  • Do not submit listings in bulk or use automated tools to do so.
  • Do not submit duplicate listings for the same website.

6. What happens if rules are broken

We may remove or hide a listing at any time, with or without warning, whether or not we checked it before it went live. Listings are not endorsements, and we do not review every site in advance.

  • A listing removed for breaking these rules is not refunded, as set out in our Terms.
  • Repeated or serious breaches may lead to you being blocked from the Site.
  • Where the law requires it, we may report unlawful content to the authorities.

7. Reporting a listing

If you see a listing that breaks these rules, tell us through our Contact page. Please include the listing’s website address and a short note on what is wrong. We review reports and act on those that break the rules.

8. Disagree with a removal?

If you think we removed your listing by mistake, write to us through the Contact page with the listing’s website address and why you think it follows the rules. We will take another look.

9. Changes

We may update these rules from time to time. The date at the top shows when they were last changed.
