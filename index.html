    Questions

    Does a higher rank mean more visitors?

    A higher rank puts your listing nearer the top of the page, where more visitors see it first. We can’t promise visitors, clicks or sales, because that depends on your product and your audience.

    What am I paying for?

    A higher position on Peakfy. Your position lasts until other listings rank above it.

    What if someone ranks above me?

    Your listing moves down as others boost theirs. Add to your total at any time to move back up.

    Can I get a refund?

    Not once your listing is live, except for a duplicate or mistaken charge, or if we remove your listing for a reason that isn’t your fault. See the Refund Policy.

    Can someone else pay for my listing?

    Yes. Payments can only raise a listing’s total, never lower it.

    What can’t be listed?

    Scams, malware, adult or illegal content and more. See the Rules. We remove listings that break them.

    Are clicks or visitors guaranteed?

    No. A higher rank gives your listing a more prominent position, but we do not guarantee any number of clicks, visitors or sales. Results depend on factors outside our control, such as your product, how you describe it, and how many people visit Peakfy. See our Terms for details.

    Who processes payments?

    Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, our payment provider, which acts as the merchant of record for your purchase. You enter your payment details on Dodo’s secure checkout page, and Peakfy never sees or stores your card details. Any applicable taxes are shown at checkout.
